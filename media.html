Healthy Food

Healthy food provides essential nutrients like vitamins, minerals, protein, and fiber, focusing on whole foods such as fruits, vegetables, whole grains, lean proteins (fish, poultry, beans, nuts), and low-fat dairy while limiting processed items, sugar, salt, and unhealthy fats to support overall health and prevent chronic diseases. Key examples include berries, leafy greens, salmon, eggs, yogurt, avocados, and legumes.
